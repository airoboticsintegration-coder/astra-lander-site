Astra Lander Support

Astra Lander is available free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. The opening chapter is included; one $2.99 purchase unlocks the full campaign. Gameplay works offline, while App Store access is required to buy, restore, or refresh the Full Campaign entitlement.

Quick help

  • If controls stop responding, pause and resume the game or restart the app.
  • Enable tilt access from the opening screen before using tilt controls.
  • Use Settings → God Mode to test later campaign levels without dying.
  • Deleting the app removes local settings, earned gameplay unlocks, scores, and pilot names. A Full Campaign purchase remains associated with the purchasing Apple Account; use Restore Purchase in Options after reinstalling.

Purchase help

  • Confirm the device is signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase.
  • Connect to the App Store, open Options, and tap Restore Purchase.
  • A pending Ask-to-Buy purchase unlocks after Apple reports the approved entitlement; reopen the app or use Restore Purchase if needed.
