Astra Lander Accessibility

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Astra Lander is a visual physics and action game. This page describes available controls and assists honestly and records known limitations. The final Accessibility Nutrition Labels will claim only features verified across all common gameplay tasks on physical iPhone hardware.

Controls and difficulty

  • On-screen touch controls
  • Optional device tilt plus thrust
  • Hybrid tilt and button controls
  • Keyboard controls in supported browser/desktop contexts
  • Easy, Normal, Hard, and Expert difficulty
  • Pure-physics, auto-level, landing-assist, and training modes
  • Limited or unlimited fuel
  • Sandbox practice
  • Optional God Mode with invincibility and forgiving landings
  • Independent music, sound-effects, and haptics controls

Visual interface

Important flight values use text labels and numbers in addition to color. Targets, controls, weapons, warnings, and rewards use shapes, icons, position, and text as well as color. A final contrast/color-dependence audit remains required on physical devices.

Known limitations

Do not interpret this page as a claim of full VoiceOver accessibility.
  • The central physics/action gameplay is rendered on a canvas and is not currently playable without vision.
  • VoiceOver and Voice Control support for every common gameplay task has not been verified.
  • The game does not currently provide 200% text scaling across all gameplay HUD elements.
  • A dedicated reduced-motion/flashing option is not yet available; explosions, particles, scrolling backgrounds, warning effects, and camera shake occur during play.
  • Audio descriptions are not provided.
  • There is no spoken dialogue requiring captions; gameplay status is primarily visual and text-based.

Planned pre-release evaluation

The TestFlight plan includes VoiceOver, Voice Control, text size, contrast, color differentiation, motion/flashing, touch targets, orientation, sound dependence, and control-mode testing. Unsupported labels will remain unclaimed.
